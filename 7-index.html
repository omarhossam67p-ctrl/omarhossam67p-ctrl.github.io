HOW I APPROACH A PROJECT

Good design is more than a good-looking image.

A clear process keeps the idea strong from the first reference to the final presentation.

01 / DISCOVERUnderstand the brief

Clarify the goal, audience, message and visual direction.

02 / EXPLOREBuild the concept

Develop references, composition and a focused creative idea.

03 / CREATEDesign with detail

Refine typography, imagery, color and visual consistency.

04 / DELIVERPrepare the outcome

Present polished visuals and production-aware final artwork.
